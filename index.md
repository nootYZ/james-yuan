Love Through Everyday Care and Connection at UCSB

This visual narrative exhibition explores how love appears in everyday student life at UCSB. Instead of focusing only on romantic love, my project looks at love through small actions, shared passion, friendship, and peer support.

Through my field observations, I noticed love in moments such as a student opening a dorm door, students gathering at a fighting game event, friends playing Werewolf in the dorm, and one roommate helping another with economics homework. These moments show that love at UCSB can be quiet, ordinary, and practical, but still meaningful.
